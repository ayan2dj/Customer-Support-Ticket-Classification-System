### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory Data Analysis was performed to understand the distribution and characteristics of the customer support ticket dataset.

The dataset contains 250 records distributed across five categories: Login Issue, Application Error, Report, Account Update and Performance.

**Key observations:**

1. Each category contains 50 tickets, indicating a balanced category distribution.
2. Low and Medium priority tickets each contain 85 records, while High priority tickets contain 80 records.
3. The status distribution consists of 85 Open, 85 In Progress and 80 Closed tickets.
4. Category-wise priority distribution is almost uniform.
5. No single category dominates the dataset.
