### Machine Learning Model

A supervised machine-learning approach was selected to classify customer support tickets based on their descriptions.

**Feature Extraction: TF-IDF**

TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) was used to convert textual ticket descriptions into numerical feature vectors. It assigns importance to words based on their frequency within a document and their distribution across the dataset.

Unigrams and bigrams were considered using the `ngram_range=(1, 2)` parameter.

**Algorithms Selected**

Two classification algorithms were implemented:

1. Logistic Regression: Selected as the primary candidate because it is computationally efficient, supports multiclass classification and works effectively with sparse text features.
2. Multinomial Naive Bayes: Selected as a comparison model because of its efficiency and suitability for text classification.

**Model Architecture**

The implementation uses Scikit-learn Pipelines to combine TF-IDF feature extraction and classification.

**Prediction Process**

A new ticket description is passed through the same preprocessing and feature extraction pipeline. The trained classifier then predicts the most likely ticket category.

The final model will be selected based on its performance on unseen test data.
